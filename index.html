Crowd Sprint

Grow your crowd, choose number gates, collect coins and take on rival teams across a sunny island runway.

Email supportPrivacy policy

How to play

  1. Tap Play. Drag your finger left or right to steer.
  2. Choose addition and multiplication gates to grow your crowd.
  3. Collect coins and reach rival teams with enough runners to win.
  4. Finish levels to earn rewards and unlock crowd colors.

Help

The pause button stops a run and offers Resume, Restart and Main Menu. Sound can be switched off on the home screen. Progress is saved locally; removing the game or clearing its storage may delete progress. The game works offline and has no account, advertisements or in-app purchases.

If something goes wrong, email itzdan4life@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, game version and the steps that caused the problem. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.
