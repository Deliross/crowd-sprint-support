Crowd Sprint support

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Crowd Sprint 1.0.0

Crowd Sprint is published by Daniele Psaila. This policy describes the game and this support website.

Data in the game

Crowd Sprint does not require an account. The game does not send gameplay progress or personal data to the publisher and does not include advertisements, behavioral analytics, in-app purchases, chat or cloud saves.

Progress and preferences, including levels, wins, coins, unlocked crowd colors and sound settings, are stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its local storage may delete that information. We do not receive it and cannot restore it. Device backups are governed by your Apple account and device settings.

Permissions and tracking

The game has no camera, microphone, location, contacts or advertising-identifier feature. It does not track users across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to answer your request and investigate problems. Messages are handled through our email provider and retained only as needed for support and applicable recordkeeping. You can request deletion by contacting the address below, subject to any legal retention obligations.

Apple and TestFlight

Apple may process App Store, device, installation and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy and your settings. If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple may share beta feedback and diagnostics with the developer. TestFlight’s data practices are separate from local game progress.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, forms or third-party fonts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect its hosting service, under the GitHub privacy statement.

Children and families

The game is intended for a general audience, including families. It does not intentionally collect personal information from children. Parents can contact us about a support message sent by a child.

Changes and contact

If data practices change, we will update this policy before releasing the affected version. For support, privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support messages, contact itzdan4life@gmail.com.
